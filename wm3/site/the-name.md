WM3 originally meant "What Made Max Mad?" — three M's, courtesy of the Mad Max moniker, and a running list of things that set Max off. Unclear instructions, mostly.

Over time he changed the framing to a better question: "What Made Max?" This site is the attempt to answer it. WM3 is also the name of the archive interface on wm3.ai — an interface to the archive, not a digital Max.
