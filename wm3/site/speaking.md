Killing the Cloud Sandbox — SRE Day 2026, Seattle and Austin, invited. How LILO moved language runtimes and AI inference off cloud servers and into the student's browser, eliminating per-execution cost: the cost model, the Java challenge, and the on-device AI economics. The talk video is at https://youtu.be/uVoWMcnwAAE and the slides are on the Speaking page.

AI Resumes That Land Interviews — Techsgiving Workshop, December 2025, invited. A 15-minute workflow for tailoring a resume to a job description without fabricating a single metric.

Bio for programs: Maxwell Myers is the co-founder and CEO of LinkedInOrLeftOut, where he is building LILO — coding education that runs language runtimes and an AI tutor entirely in the browser. He spent five years in and around Microsoft Edge — three internships, then full-time on the Web Platform team — leading the WebView2 Find on Page API end-to-end and working upstream in Chromium's media pipeline. He studied computer science at the University of Maryland, where he also captained the Division I track and field team.
