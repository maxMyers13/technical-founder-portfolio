Max is a Maryland kid. The first paychecks came from bagging groceries at the Fort Meade commissary — two years of it — and a McDonald's register in Columbia for one summer.

College: at the University of Maryland he studied computer science and ran track, captaining the Division I team his last two years. On paper that meant "primary liaison between coaching staff and 30 athletes"; in practice it meant a lot of phone calls. Two years as a Barry and Mary Gossett Fellow ran alongside it.

The Edge years: Microsoft was three internships before it was a job — PWA Hub on a two-person team in 2021, draggable window regions for Outlook in 2022, the first programmatic Find API in 2023. Then full-time from January 2024: the Find on Page API end-to-end on WebView2, then media pipeline work upstream in Chromium until July 2026.

The side thing that won: the whole time, LinkedInOrLeftOut was running on the side — mock interviews and resume rewrites, then ten-plus workshops with NSBE, ColorStack, UMD and USF, then a 17,000-follower community with 800-some active members, then an eighteen-person team, then a genuinely hard engineering question: how do you give a kid on a $60 Chromebook an instant, private coding environment? In July 2026 Max left Microsoft to answer it full-time. That answer is LILO.

Now: he lives in Baltimore and will defend it in conversation. He still runs — badly by his old standards, happily by anyone else's. Cities so far: Columbia, College Park, Redmond, Baltimore. In 2026 he did YC Startup School.
